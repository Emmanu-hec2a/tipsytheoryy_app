# Roadmap: Empowering "Base Plan" Stores

While Pro Stores get the "Elite" treatment, Base Plan merchants need a solid, professional foundation to run their business effectively. I recommend these "Essential" features to keep them competitive:

## 1. Professional Foundation
- **"Standard Partner" Badge:** Instead of the jagged teal seal, Base stores get a clean, circular light-grey or forest-green "Partner" tick. It still shows they are verified, but lacks the "Pro" prestige.
- **Basic Store Analytics:** Access to a "Simple Stats" dashboard showing daily revenue and most popular items (Pro stores would get deep hourly/demographic insights).

## 2. Operational Efficiency
- **Scheduled Store Hours:** A robust "Opening/Closing Hours" manager so they don't get orders when they are closed.
- **Instant "Out of Stock" Toggle:** A one-tap button on their product list to hide items that are temporarily unavailable.

## 3. Customer Engagement (Limited)
- **"New Arrival" Tags:** Allow Base stores to mark up to 3 items as "New" to draw attention to fresh inventory.
- **Standard Promotional Codes:** Ability to create basic "KSh 100 Off" or "5% Discount" codes for their specific store.

## 4. Platform Fairness
- **Distance-Based Visibility:** While Pro stores get priority everywhere, Base stores should still appear prominently to users within a **tight 2km radius** to ensure local shops aren't "buried" in their own neighborhood.

---
### **Recommendation for Next Steps:**
I suggest we implement the **Standard Partner Badge** and the **Opening/Closing Hours Manager** first to ensure all stores look professional and function reliably.
