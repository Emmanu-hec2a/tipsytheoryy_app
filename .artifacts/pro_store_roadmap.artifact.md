# Roadmap: Elevating "Pro Stores" on TipsyTheoryy

To make "Pro Store" status truly valuable for merchants and distinctive for customers, I recommend the following "Elite" features:

## 1. Visual Prestige (Immediate Impact)
- **Gold-Bordered Cards:** In the main feed, Pro Stores should have a subtle gradient gold border or a "Premium" sash in the corner.
- **Custom Brand Headers:** Allow Pro Stores to have a "Cover Image" behind their logo in the Store Detail screen (instead of the plain green gradient).

## 2. Platform Priority (Merchant Value)
- **Priority Search Ranking:** Pro Stores should always appear at the top of search results and the "Stores Near You" list.
- **"Pro Verified" Filter:** Add a toggle in the home screen to "Only show Pro Stores" for users who want guaranteed quality.

## 3. Trust & Reliability (Customer Confidence)
- **Express Delivery Badge:** If a Pro store averages <20 mins, add a "Lightning Bolt" icon next to the Pro badge.
- **Trust Banner at Checkout:** When a user orders from a Pro store, show a small banner: *"You are ordering from a Pro-Verified Merchant. Quality and speed are guaranteed."*

## 4. Enhanced Content
- **Video Hero:** Allow Pro stores to upload a 5-second "Looping Video" as their banner (e.g., someone pouring a drink).
- **Featured Items Gallery:** A dedicated "Best Sellers" horizontal scroll at the very top of their menu.

---
### **Which of these should we implement next to make "Pro" feel like a real upgrade?**
I recommend starting with **Priority Ranking** and the **Gold-Bordered Card Style**.
